About PowerSmart

Built in Kenya, for Kenyan power

PowerSmart Kenya is a prepaid electricity management platform that helps households, tenants and landlords monitor meters, buy tokens instantly and never lose power.

⚡

Made for Kenya Power meters

Works with your existing prepaid meter account — no hardware, installation or extra wiring required.

🤝

Trusted by tenants & landlords

From single apartments to entire blocks, we help everyone keep the lights on and manage their power simply.

🛠️

Simple to get started

Create an account with your meter number and start buying tokens in under a minute.

What we offer

One platform for every power challenge

From single-meter tenants to landlords running entire apartment blocks.

📟

Real-time meter telemetry

Track your kWh balance and daily consumption live, with depletion forecasts so you always know when to top up.

💳

Instant token vending

Buy tokens with M-Pesa, Airtel Money or bank transfer. Tokens are generated and pushed to your meter automatically.

🏢

Landlord fleet tools

Manage multiple meters, keep a complete token and payment ledger, and configure automatic top-up rules.

🔔

Smart low-balance alerts

Get notified by push, email or SMS before your power runs out — with recommended top-up amounts.

📊

Usage analytics

Understand where your power goes with appliance cost estimates and daily consumption trends.

🛡️

Bank-grade security

Encrypted passwords, signed sessions and role-based access keep your meter account and payments safe.
